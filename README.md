# Chinese Four-Character Blessings · 四字吉语词典

An open bilingual dictionary of **124 Chinese four-character blessings** (四字吉语) — the auspicious phrases found on seals, calligraphy, New Year decorations, and gifts. Each entry has the original Chinese, an English translation, a category, and a short explanation of its meaning and when it's used.

一個開放的**四字吉語雙語詞典**：124 句常見於印章、書法、春聯與禮物的吉祥話，每條含中文原文、英譯、分類與用法釋義。

## Why four characters? / 為什麼是四個字？

Chinese auspicious phrases are almost always four characters (四字). The rhythm is memorable, the meaning is dense, and the form fits perfectly on a seal, a scroll, or a red paper couplet. This collection covers the most-loved ones, grouped by the wish behind them:

中國吉祥話幾乎都是四個字——音節好記、寓意濃縮、剛好能刻進印章或寫在春聯上。本合集按祝願的主題分類：

- **life / 生活平安** — 福星高照，心想事成，安居樂業， Auspicious Star, Peaceful Home
- **wealth / 財富興旺** — 招財進寶，金玉滿堂，日進斗金，Summon Wealth, Gold and Jade
- **career / 事業前程** — 前程似錦，步步高升，紫微星耀，Bright Future, Rising Step by Step
- **love / 感情姻緣** — 琴瑟和鳴，花好月圓，白頭偕老，Harmony, Reunion, Growing Old Together
